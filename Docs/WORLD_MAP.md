# World Map Plan

## Region 1: San Veyra, USA — first production target
A fictional coastal city intended to become the first major open-world region.

### Planned districts
1. Downtown — dense commercial skyline and central missions.
2. Vice Coast — beaches, hotels, nightlife, and waterfront roads.
3. Industrial Zone — factories, warehouses, and faction activity.
4. Old Town — compact streets and older architecture.
5. Airport District — civilian airport and aircraft facilities.
6. Military District — restricted high-security area.
7. Harbor — docks, container yards, fishing boats, and cargo missions.
8. Suburbs — residential streets and safehouse opportunities.

### First blockout requirements
- Coastline and navigable water boundary
- Main road loop and secondary street grid
- District landmarks and readable silhouettes
- Harbor, airport, and one safehouse
- Terrain height variation and at least one major bridge
- Streaming cells sized after profiling, not guessed permanently

## Region 2: Isla Mar
Alex's small island home. It should contrast with San Veyra: fishing village, docks, winding roads, hills, modest homes, and a few remote coastal locations.

## Region 3: Nevada, USA
Desert highways, isolated research facilities, military checkpoints, and Area 09.

## Region 4: New York, USA
A later urban region, scoped only after the first city is playable.

## Region 5: Moscow, Russia
A later international story region, scoped only after the first city is playable.

## Streaming strategy
Use World Partition for the large landscape where appropriate, HLOD for distant representations, and Data Layers for mission-specific or outbreak-state content. Travel between far-separated regions should use controlled transitions or separate world packages rather than loading every region simultaneously.
