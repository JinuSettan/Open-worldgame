# Game Design Overview

## High concept
WORLD: Rise of the Underworld is a single-player, third-person open-world action story. The player follows Alex from a small island into the criminal and political power struggles of a fictionalised modern world.

## Player fantasy
Explore a living city, build relationships, drive and fly across a large world, take part in consequential missions, and decide whether power will be used for control or to restore civilian freedom.

## Core loop
1. Explore a district and discover activities.
2. Gather information, equipment, vehicles, and allies.
3. Choose and complete a mission or side activity.
4. Affect faction standing, story state, and access to locations.
5. Return to a safehouse, save progress, and unlock the next opportunity.

## Core systems
- Third-person movement and camera
- Vehicle entry/exit and driving
- Boats and aircraft as later milestones
- Mission objectives and checkpoints
- NPC schedules and simplified distant simulation
- Faction relationships
- Police awareness and wanted escalation
- Infected enemy encounters
- Shops, safehouses, properties, and side missions
- Save/load and story-choice persistence

## Scope rules
- San Veyra is the first playable region.
- The first build is a small but coherent vertical slice, not the entire world.
- Greybox geometry is acceptable until traversal, scale, streaming, and performance are proven.
- All factions are fictional; avoid direct recreation of real criminal organisations.
- Violence and outbreak content must serve the story and gameplay, not replace them.

## Target platforms
PC and Android share core gameplay concepts, but use different scalability profiles. Android requires reduced scene complexity, careful memory budgets, scalable effects, and device testing.
