# Story Bible

## Main characters
- **Alex:** young man from Isla Mar; the player protagonist.
- **Russell:** Viktor's trusted right-hand man; becomes Alex's guide and mentor.
- **Viktor:** leader of Black Crown; saves Alex from poverty by offering him a place in the organisation.
- **Maya:** Alex's love interest. Her abduction and death become a major emotional turning point.
- **Victor Hale:** corrupt police commander connected to secret experiments and the outbreak.

## Organisations
- **Black Crown:** fictional international criminal organisation.
- **Red Vipers:** rival gang.
- **Silent Order:** covert rival network.
- **Iron Wolves:** armed faction.
- **Meridian Cartel:** international criminal rival.

## Story premise
Alex struggles to survive on Isla Mar. A sea accident and near-drowning convince him to leave the island to seek work. In a foreign city, Russell is waiting. During an ambush against Viktor, Alex helps save Viktor and drives him to safety. Viktor offers Alex a place in Black Crown.

Alex's work takes him across ports, rival territories, and international operations. Later, missing scientists and a secret Nevada facility reveal a programme of human experimentation. Corrupt commander Victor Hale has been using infected subjects and seeks to turn the crisis into political power.

Maya is abducted and killed during the experiments. Alex's loyalty fractures. He works to expose the programme, stop the outbreak, and decide what should replace the system that enabled it.

## Five-act outline
1. **The Stranger (01–10):** Isla Mar, sea accident, departure, Russell, ambush, Viktor rescue, invitation, first assignment.
2. **The Rise (11–20):** rival factions, port operations, aircraft, informants, Viktor's past, betrayal, growing US influence.
3. **The Virus (21–30):** missing scientists, Area 09 Nevada, Patient Zero, infected precinct, Hale revealed, quarantine, survivors, cure research, Maya abducted and killed.
4. **Revenge (31–40):** Alex challenges Black Crown, Russell's loyalty, drug empire, human experiments, underground facility, evidence, cure, Hale's forces, war for America.
5. **The King (41–50):** fall of Hale, final lab, last infection, Black Crown's fate, Russell's final choice, new order, public voice, broadcast, final night, ending.

## Endings
- **The King:** Alex takes control; the ending examines the cost of concentrated power.
- **Freedom:** Alex helps establish a civilian-led future and gives power back to the people.
- **The Last Virus:** the outbreak is not fully contained, leaving a darker unresolved future.

The exact conditions and mission choices for each ending will be designed during mission scripting.
